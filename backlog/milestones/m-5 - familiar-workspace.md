## Description

Lamina's interface rearranged into the layout people already know from Photoshop, Affinity and similar editors: tools at the left in groups with flyouts, a context options bar above the canvas, Properties and Adjustments docked over Layers at the right, menus in the conventional order with the established names, and dialogs with settings on the left and buttons on the right. It follows the macOS light or dark appearance and fits a 1500 × 860 pt window (a 14-inch MacBook Pro). Planned features show their controls in place and say they are in progress when used.

The maintained spec is docs/DESIGN.md; the approved mockup is docs/references/redesign_v2.html; the policy is decision-9. This milestone comes before any other new feature work (fixes and releases can still ship).
