Lamina Essentials workspace

The editor rearranged around Photoshop 2026's layout (v27.11): its toolbar groups, options bars, menus, panels and dialogs, with Photoshop's names, showing only what Lamina can already do, in light and dark. The window below is exactly 1500 × 860 points, the usable area on a 14-inch MacBook Pro.

Appearance
Moved Renamed or restyled Split into its own tool or item New wiring for an existing feature Shortcut Lamina only

Click a tool; hold it or right-click for its group. Tool keys work too (V, M, L, W, C, I, J, B, S, E, G, O, T, U, H, Z; Shift cycles a group). Menus open from the bar above the window; items ending in “…” open their dialog. Click layers, the mask thumbnail, panel tabs, the History icon, and the transform box on the canvas. Hover a dot for what changed.
